# Upwork Portföy – AI Real Estate Assistant

**Title (≤70):**
AI Real Estate Chatbot & Lead Qualification with n8n

**Role:** AI Automation Developer (n8n)

**Description (≤600):**
I built an AI chat assistant for real estate agencies using n8n and an AI Agent. It answers property questions only from your own Google Sheet listings (no made-up properties), asks one friendly question at a time, scores every lead from 0 to 100 and saves it to a Leads sheet. Qualified leads get three viewing slots and the chosen time is written back automatically. It replies in English or German and remembers the conversation. Fast to set up, cheap to run on Groq. I can adapt it to your CRM, website chat or WhatsApp.

**5 Skills:** n8n · AI Agents · Chatbot Development · Google Sheets API · Lead Generation

**3 Ekran görüntüsü:**
1. n8n canvas – 8 node'un tamamı görünür (`docs/canvas.png`)
2. Chat – listing sonuçları + saat önerisi (`docs/chat.png`)
3. Leads sheet – puan ve ViewingSlot dolu satırlar (`docs/leads-sheet.png`)
